# ⚡ QUICK FIX - Load Chrome Extension

## 🎯 3-Step Solution:

### ✅ Step 1: Open Chrome Extensions
Type in Chrome address bar: **`chrome://extensions/`**

### ✅ Step 2: Enable & Load
1. Turn **"Developer mode"** ON (top-right toggle)
2. Click **"Load unpacked"** button
3. Select folder: `C:\Users\sanke\OneDrive\Desktop\Taskpilot AI\extension`

### ✅ Step 3: Go Back to TaskPilot
Go to: **`http://localhost:3002/`**

**The warning will automatically disappear in 1-2 seconds!** ✨

---

## 🎉 What Happens:

❌ **Before:** Yellow warning: "Chrome Extension Not Detected"  
✅ **After:** Warning disappears, "Share Screen" button works!

---

## 💡 Pro Tips:

- **Don't see "Load unpacked"?** → Turn Developer mode ON
- **Still showing warning?** → Click the **"🔄 Check Again"** button in the warning
- **Want to verify?** → Press F12, look for: `✅ TaskPilot Extension detected and ready!`

---

## 🚀 Then Test:

1. Click Live AI Robot (bottom-right)
2. Select "Live Voice"  
3. Warning should be **GONE**
4. Click "Share Screen" → Works! 🎊

**That's it!** 3 steps and you're done! 🚀
