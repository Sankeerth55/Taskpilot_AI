## 🎯 HOW TO USE:

### Step 1: Open TaskPilot
Go to: **http://localhost:3002/**

### Step 2: Start Live Voice Mode
1. Click the **Live AI Robot** (bottom-right)
2. Select **"Live Voice"**
3. Wait for "Active" status

### Step 3: Share Your Screen
1. Click **"Share Screen"** button (always enabled!)
2. Choose what to share:
   - **Entire Screen** (recommended)
   - **Window**
   - **Chrome Tab**
3. Click "Share"

### Step 4: Talk to AI
**Without Extension:**
- "What do you see on my screen?"
- "Read the text in the top section"
- "What website am I on?"
- "Explain this page to me"
- "What should I do next?"

**With Extension (Optional):**
- All the above PLUS:
- "Scroll down"
- "Click the submit button"
- "Type my name is John"
- "Click the first link"
